# Business Analysis

A collection of business case studies applying BA methodology, PESTEL, stakeholder
analysis, competitor analysis, and regulatory strategy to real, current technology
and market-entry problems. Written with a South African context, connecting my
Computer Science and Business Management background to practical strategic analysis.

## Case Studies

### 📡 [Satellite Internet Market Entry: Starlink vs Amazon Leo](./satellite-internet-market-entry.md)
An analysis of why Starlink remains locked out of the South African market under
BEE ownership law, while Amazon Leo secured a 2027 launch by partnering with an
already-licensed local operator. Covers PESTEL analysis, competitor comparison,
stakeholder mapping, and a recommended market-entry strategy for new satellite
entrants.

**Skills demonstrated:** regulatory analysis, competitor benchmarking, stakeholder
mapping, market-entry strategy, evidence-based recommendation writing
